### UI/UX Enhancement Initiative

**Objective:** Perform a comprehensive audit and refactoring of the Billiard Management System interface to improve visual hierarchy, user experience (UX), and technical scalability.

**Scope of Work:**
1. **Design System Integration:** Establish a unified design language including a standardized typography scale, color palette (primary, secondary, semantic), and spacing system.
2. **Responsive Optimization:** Ensure full responsiveness across mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints, prioritizing touch-friendly interactions for table management.
3. **Accessibility (a11y):** Implement WCAG 2.1 compliance standards, focusing on high-contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, and semantic HTML structure.
4. **Performance & Feedback:** Optimize asset loading and integrate micro-interactions (loading states, success/error toasts) to provide immediate user feedback.
5. **Workflow Streamlining:** Identify and eliminate friction points in the reservation and billing modules to reduce cognitive load for staff members.
